Set is a simple Markdown based note-taking app. Your notes are stored on your device and sync directly between them.

Set is open-source and available on Mac, Windows and Linux.

[Download the app](https://writewithset.com?download=true) · [GitHub](https://github.com/rootstring/set)

> [!NOTE]
> You can use Set in your browser and your notes are saved in this browser only. If you clear its data, they're gone. Export them from **Settings → Storage** to keep a copy or [download the desktop app](https://writewithset.com?download=true).

## Features

- **Stored as Markdown:** Every note is a plain Markdown file on your computer that any app can open. You can choose where to store the notes locally.

- **Sync your devices:** You can sync between your devices without an account or a server.

- **Make it yours:** Light and dark themes, custom colors, fonts and shortcuts.

- **Blocks:** Block based headings, lists, to-dos, tables, images and more with an optional `/` command. Drag blocks around to rearrange them.

- **Pages and contexts:** Put pages inside pages, and use contexts as workspaces to keep notes separate.

- **Quick switcher:** Press {{quickSwitcher}} to search pages, content or execute commands.

- **Dictation:** Set can transcribe your speech into text on your device.

- **Agent Access:** Choose whether AI assistants can search, read or add to your notes.
